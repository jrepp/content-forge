# Ingot source family

The family definition and retained Blockbench source are GPL-3.0-or-later,
Copyright (C) 2026 Jacob Repp. The embedded pixels start from content-forge's
existing iron/gold ingot recipe exemplars, not third-party pack artwork.
`source-provenance.json` records their exact hashes and the initial source hash.

This two-item family proves the author/export/review workflow. It is candidate
content; neither the initial pixels nor the display transforms have received new
visual or in-game acceptance. The editable `.bbmodel` is authoritative after
initial authoring. Export must not regenerate or overwrite it from the seed.
